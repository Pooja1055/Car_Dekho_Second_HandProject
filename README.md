**Second-Hand Car Dealer SQL Project**

**Overview**

This SQL project helps a second-hand car dealer manage and analyze their inventory data. It includes several SQL queries to answer key questions about car availability, fuel types, and inventory by year.

**Data Source**

The project uses a database containing details about second-hand cars, including:

Car make, model, year, and fuel type

Car availability and pricing

**Key Queries**

The project answers the following questions:

**Total Cars:** Count the total number of cars in the inventory.

**Cars in 2023:** Count how many cars are available in 2023.

**Cars in 2020, 2021, 2022:** Count how many cars are available in these years.

**Total Cars by Year:** Summary of car count by year.

**Diesel Cars in 2020:** Count of diesel cars in 2020.

**Petrol Cars in 2020:** Count of petrol cars in 2020.

**All Fuel Types by Year:** List of cars by fuel type (Petrol, Diesel, CNG) for each year.

**Years with More Than 100 Cars:** Identify which years had more than 100 cars.

**Cars Between 2015 and 2023:** Complete list of cars available from 2015 to 2023.

**Technologies Used**

MySQL: For managing and querying the car inventory database.

SQL: For writing queries to retrieve and analyze the data.

**Conclusion**

This project provides a second-hand car dealer with important insights into their inventory, such as total car count, available cars by year, and breakdowns by fuel type. It helps answer customer and management queries and assists in inventory management.
